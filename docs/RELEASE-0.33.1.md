# Hard Play 0.33.1

- Nano wheel uses fixed, symmetric cardinal positions: Open above, playlist/unlock below, Previous/Next to the sides, Play in the center. Its minimum height is 380 px to reserve room for the display and controls.
- Main/Mini playlist management uses one horizontal row: Save, Rename, New, Delete and Add Audio.
- Dedicated Crossfade Settings offers Off/0, 3 and 6 seconds, with explicit Apply to This Transition and Apply to Entire Playlist. Changes to all transitions can be undone.
- Nano and the menu-bar controller show elapsed/remaining time in minutes and seconds, total current-track duration and playlist track count. Waveform seeking remains available.

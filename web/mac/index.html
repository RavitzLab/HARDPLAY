01 / PREVIEW

Listen ahead. Keep the main output clear.

Send playback to your main system and audition tracks through a second audio output. Check what comes next before it reaches your audience.

02 / TRANSITIONS

Make every handover intentional.

Set a crossfade for one transition or apply it to the entire playlist. Choose a clean cut, stop at the end, repeat a track or playlist, or turn on shuffle.

03 / AUDIO EDITING

Prepare the part you actually need.

Zoom into the waveform, select and trim sections, join regions, and detect silence at the edges. Export a new WAV or replace the playlist entry while keeping the original file.

04 / LINKED PLAYBACK

Two Macs. A shared set.

Pair Primary A and Backup B on your local network. Sync the playlist and its settings, then enable Linked Playback on both Macs to share Play/Pause, track changes and seeks.

READY FOR A LIVE SESSION

Protect your set from accidental clicks.

Lock Controls

Click the padlock to disable player controls. Playback continues. Hold the same button for 1.5 seconds to unlock; releasing early cancels the gesture. In Nano, use the bottom wheel button to unlock.

Setup protection

Protect playlist edits, track selection, playback controls and settings separately. Choose what stays available during your session.

Waveform seek lock

Unlock one seek with the small padlock beside the track time. It locks again when you release the waveform, or after 5 seconds without a seek.

FROM DOWNLOAD TO FIRST PLAY

Five steps. Then press Play.

  1. Install. Open the DMG and drag HARDPLAY.app into Applications.
  2. Add your audio. Drop files into the player or playlist. Selecting a track does not start playback; use its Play button.
  3. Choose your outputs. Open PATCH → Audio. Set Main Output and a separate Preview Output.
  4. Prepare your playlist. Arrange tracks, set transitions, and save your set.
  5. Connect a second Mac, if needed. Load the same audio files on both Macs. Pair them in Timecode & Backup, sync the set, and enable Linked Playback on both.

Before your first session

HARDPLAY is currently a beta. Test your playlist, audio interface and network before using it live. Linked Playback shares transport commands; it is not sample-accurate hardware sync or automatic audio failover. Audio files must exist on both Macs. Output routing and volume remain local.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, verify the download source and use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. You do not need to disable macOS security.
